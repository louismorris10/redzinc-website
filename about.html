Home/About us

A telemedicine company based in Dublin, Ireland.

RedZinc Services Limited is a software-as-a-service company specialising in web-based, real-time video communication solutions for healthcare.

RedZinc’s BlueEye integrated remote monitoring solution supports virtual-ward and Hospital-at-Home services by combining compatible connected sensors, a patient application and a cloud-based clinician dashboard. The platform transmits and presents supported physiological measurements for professional review and supports video consultations where configured.

It is designed for hospital-at-home services, combining wearable sensors, a patient smartphone app and a cloud-based hotdesk for automated patient monitoring, with live video consultations through the BlueEye video call feature.

Our vision and values

RedZinc’s vision is to bring mobile telemedicine into a new era, improving the lives of patients and healthcare professionals while enhancing operational efficiency. It also aims to transform global healthcare through integrated care, using a scalable cloud-based telemedicine platform that connects patients and professionals anywhere.

That vision is in line with the EU Research and Innovation Programme goals of securing Europe’s global competitiveness and tackling societal challenges such as escalating healthcare costs in an ageing population. RedZinc has taken part in or managed European research projects to advance eHealth applications and network technology, including hospital at home, real-time video communications and wearable video.

The research and development covers 5G/6G networks, quality of service, video capabilities, real-time data streaming, network slicing and software-defined networks, and has resulted in advanced technologies and new products in telemedicine and real-time video communications.

Our international presence.

Three European research projects that shaped the BlueEye products.

Ongoing

6G-PATH: 6G pilots and trials throughout Europe

RedZinc is involved in the 6G-PATH project, contributing to XR (extended reality) education research and developing its video technology and real-time data streaming for advanced networks.

Description. The project fosters development and integration of tools and products from EU companies with 5G/6G, and measures key performance indicators and key value indicators. Seven testbeds are used by ten use cases across four verticals: health, education, smart cities and farming. RedZinc contributes to 6G connectivity and usability in healthcare.

Objective. Set up the 6G-PATH experiment, infrastructure and testbeds, involve user communities in large-scale field trials through Open Calls, gather requirements from the verticals, and devise business models for commercialising and exploiting the use cases.

Outcomes so far.

  • The BlueEye platform is deployed on 6G testbeds to experiment with more advanced, commercially unavailable network technology.
  • Validation of key performance indicators such as uplink, latency and packet loss, and key value indicators such as user satisfaction, enhanced eHealth education and scalability, in real-world scenarios.
  • Valuable user feedback that shapes improvements to the systems and solutions.

6G-PATH has received funding from the Smart Networks and Services Joint Undertaking (SNS JU) under the European Union’s Horizon Europe research and innovation programme under Grant Agreement No 101139172. Co-funded by the European Union. Learn more about 6G-PATH.

2020–2021

Celtic-Next Health 5G: innovative clinical pilots

RedZinc took an active part in the HEALTH-5G Celtic-Next Eureka Cluster project, piloting three new clinical uses of mobile video telemedicine in Sweden, Germany and Turkey.

Description. HEALTH-5G is a pan-European collaboration on new 5G health applications. It identifies novel eHealth use cases that use 5G capabilities, develops 5G enablers, validates eHealth solutions on real 5G test networks, and disseminates results.

Outcomes. Three showcases and pilots delivered new insight into clinical use cases:

  • Out-of-hours nurse support for frail elderly people in Sweden
  • Cardio ultrasound teaching in Germany
  • Earthquake disaster recovery in Turkey

Pilot feedback helped RedZinc evolve its real-time video stack in features, stability and robustness, and develop new cameras. RedZinc thanks Enterprise Ireland and Dublin City Council LEO for their support (Agile project support). Learn more about Health 5G.

€14.3m EU-funded

5G-HEART: digital use cases in healthcare, transport and aquaculture

5G-HEART is a €14.3m EU-funded public-private partnership for new 5G use cases. The bid was co-led by RedZinc, Telenor, Philips and VTT. RedZinc has a contract with the project to deliver wearable video pilots in Norway, Finland and the Netherlands.

Emergency services, Netherlands

With Ambulancezorg Groningen, in-field paramedics used BlueEye wearable video to send real-time point-of-view video and vital patient data to a remote medical manager, who previously could only offer voice support. The pilot concluded that point-of-view video and vital data significantly improve remote patient assessment. Watch the pilot video.

Medical education, Finland

Oulu University Hospital used BlueEye wearable video, via BlueEye Handsfree, to deliver paediatrics bedside tutorials to remote students when COVID-19 had stalled medical education. In a survey of 27 participants, more than 80% said teaching with wearable video could partly or wholly replace face-to-face teaching. Watch the pilot video.

Urban search and rescue, Norway

Oslo Ambulance service identified several uses, including situational assessment for urban search and rescue, hazard checklist management, single-vehicle paramedic support, oncology support, special needs support and pain relief drug support. The urban search and rescue pilot concluded successfully: wearable video let the remote incident commander immerse themselves in the situation, improving situational awareness and decision-making, and real-time support increased the searcher’s confidence. Watch the pilot video or read the white paper.
