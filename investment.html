RedZinc investment opportunity

RedZinc is raising capital to support sales and meet demand for its BlueEye telemedicine products. The product is built and tested, with a sales pipeline in several European countries.

Request the prospectus

€4.3m

Total invested in the company, including €2.8m from the Horizon 5G Public Private Partnership.

€5,000

Minimum investment under the scheme.

4 years

Minimum holding period.

20% relief

Tax relief under the Employment Investment Incentive Scheme (EIIS). For example, a €10,000 investment costs €8,000 net.

Company highlights

  • Based in Dublin, operating in the telemedicine sector and revenue generating
  • BlueEye is operational in Irish and UK hospitals
  • Approved as an HSE telemedicine supplier
  • Winner of a 2020 Irish Healthcare Award
  • Piloted in several European countries, with hospitals signed up to virtual ward programmes

The problem and the solution

Problem. Rising healthcare demand, staff shortages, rising costs, hospital capacity issues, long waiting lists, and emergency services relying on outdated 2G communications.

Solution. Shared care models, home hospital care and connected ambulance technology, which RedZinc says can improve response times and patient care.

Products

  • Wearable video for paramedics
  • Home hospital monitoring with wearable sensors
  • A secure video consultation platform for clinicians

Important. Investors should seek independent financial advice before proceeding with an EIIS investment. For a prospectus or presentation, contact investor@redzinc.net or +353 (1) 906 9608.
