Hospital & healthcare

Hospital and healthcare use cases include outpatients departments, community nurse, doctor on call, nursing homes and ambulance services. Video consultation and vital signs data for remote patients, or a wearable camera and patient sensors for hospital-based support.

First responders

Send real-time video and vital signs data using a wearable camera and biometric sensors to a remote expert. Use cases include paramedic buddy support, paramedic to remote expert clinician, fire, police and disaster recovery.
