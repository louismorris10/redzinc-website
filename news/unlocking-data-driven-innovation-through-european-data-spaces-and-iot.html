Unlocking data-driven innovation through European Data Spaces and IoT

How IoT data and EU common data spaces can support remote patient monitoring and keep patient data sovereign.

  • IoT devices produce large volumes of real-time data. European Data Spaces are EU-wide, interoperable data-sharing frameworks meant to reduce legal and technical barriers to using it.
  • Examples span remote patient monitoring in healthcare, asset tracking, mining safety, smart metering in energy, and crop and weather monitoring in agriculture.
  • In RedZinc's approach, wearable IoT devices send patient vital signs to clinical teams through BlueEye Virtual Ward, with end-to-end encryption, local data servers and dedicated routers.
  • RedZinc argues that European Data Spaces for healthcare protect patient privacy better than routing data through global hyperscalers, especially where data would leave the EU.
