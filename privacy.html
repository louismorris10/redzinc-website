Privacy statement

RedZinc Services Ltd. Data protection contact: dataprotection@redzinc.net.

Editor’s note: this page is an outline of the current privacy policy. Replace it with the full legal text from the live site before publishing.

Part I: where RedZinc is a data controller

Information collected

Contact, billing, correspondence and website visit details. RedZinc does not process children’s data.

How it is used

Handling enquiries, completing transactions, meeting legal duties and debt collection, among ten stated purposes. Consent is given through website acceptance or signed forms.

Retention

Data is kept securely and used only for its original purpose. The standard retention period is seven years, shorter for some documents such as unsuccessful job applicants’ CVs.

Automated decisions

RedZinc does not make automated decisions with personal data.

Sharing and transfers

Some tasks are outsourced under data processing agreements, and data may be disclosed when legally required. Transfers are mainly within the EU and to the United States.

Marketing and social media

Marketing uses explicit opt-in consent only, with no pre-ticked boxes, and consent can be withdrawn at any time. BlueEye.video data is never used for marketing or social media.

Part II: where RedZinc is a data processor

Information processed

RedZinc processes data on behalf of clients such as hospitals, healthcare workers and law enforcement through the BlueEye service, using encryption and cloud hosting. Video or audio is recorded only in limited cases.

Sub-processors and transfers

Data may be passed to sub-processors under agreements. Amazon Web Services is named as a sub-processor.

Part III: your rights

Information storage

Software protection, network checks, two-step authentication and secure physical storage. Video and audio processed through the products are not recorded or stored.

Requesting your data

Make a written or email request. Identity verification may be required, and verbal requests are not accepted.

Rectifying mistakes

Inaccurate data is corrected at no cost on written or email request.

Queries or complaints

Raise questions or complaints with RedZinc first, then the Data Protection Commission if needed.

Changes

Updates are posted on the website and take effect on posting.
