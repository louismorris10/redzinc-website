Care that carries on when the patient goes home.

RedZinc’s BlueEye integrated remote monitoring solution is designed to support virtual-ward and Hospital-at-Home services. It combines compatible connected sensors, a patient smartphone application and a cloud-based clinician dashboard, so supported measurements reach professionals for review. Video consultations are supported where configured.

Three parts, one view for the care team.

The patient measures at home. BlueEye sends supported readings to the cloud with timestamps and data status. Clinicians review them and, where configured, start a video consultation.

Connected sensors

Compatible CE-marked sensors capture physiological measurements.

Patient smartphone app

The BlueEye Care app sends measurements to the cloud and carries questionnaires and video.

Clinician dashboard

The cloud-based BlueEye Hotdesk presents measurements with timestamps and data status for professional review.
