BlueEye Virtual Ward

Remote monitoring and workflow support for virtual ward services

BlueEye Virtual Ward enables healthcare professionals to remotely review physiological measurements received from compatible CE-marked sensors within virtual-ward and Hospital-at-Home pathways.

Request a demoWatch video

Save costs and optimise hospital capacity by moving patients home.

The BlueEye Virtual Ward home hospital platform lets clinical teams remotely monitor patients in their homes, reducing hospital overcrowding and improving patient comfort.

Why BlueEye Virtual Ward?

It supports home-based remote monitoring pathways by securely transmitting and presenting supported physiological measurements for review by authorised healthcare professionals. The platform supports patient questionnaires, video communication, review documentation and care-team workflow routing, according to the healthcare organisation’s local procedures.

Patient selection, clinical review, discharge, escalation and treatment decisions remain the responsibility of the healthcare organisation.

Three components.

A kit of sensors, a patient app and a browser-based clinician workspace.

GreenBox: customisable kit of sensors

Compatible CE-marked connected sensors provide supported physiological measurements for transmission through the BlueEye Care application, and are designed to support multiple patient pathways. Supported configurations may include a CE-marked multi-vital ECG patch, SpO₂ monitor, blood-pressure cuff, infrared thermometer and weighing scale.

BlueEye Care mobile app

Patients use the app to connect supported sensors, transmit measurements, complete assigned questionnaires, view received measurement and sensor-status information, and communicate with the care team where configured.

Browser-based BlueEye Hotdesk

The clinical team securely accesses patient charts from any device with an internet connection, with no software to install. They can also onboard patients, document reviews, route review tasks within the care-team workflow and record discharge from the service, per local protocol.

Key features.

 

Automated data transmission

Compatible sensors automatically send supported measurements through the BlueEye Care app. BlueEye presents them with timestamps and available data-status information. Availability depends on sensor operation, system configuration and connectivity.

Sovereign data centre

Patient data is stored in a secure, locally hosted data centre, supporting compliance with regional regulations. The clinical team keeps control and ownership of patient information.

Customisable questionnaires

Design and deploy tailored questionnaires for patients to complete remotely. Responses are presented to authorised professionals for review according to the configured pathway.

Secure video consultations

Integrated video supports consultations, check-ins and pathway follow-up between patients and authorised professionals where configured.

Co-create with the BlueEye team

Work with the team to integrate Virtual Ward with existing clinical workflows, develop new features and tailor deployment for an effective Hospital at Home service.

Customisable pathways

Configure monitoring and follow-up workflows for patient cohorts selected by the organisation, for example step-down care after surgery or proactive disease management.

How it works.

A typical community pathway, configured by the healthcare organisation.

  1. Onboard the patientThe care team onboards the patient in the Hotdesk, and the patient receives a configured kit.
  2. Patient measures at homeThe patient uses the BlueEye Care app to send measurements and complete assigned questionnaires.
  3. Clinicians reviewAuthorised professionals review current and historical measurements, questionnaire responses and video where configured.
  4. Review promptsConfigurable, non-life-critical prompts and notifications route review tasks within the care team.
  5. Follow up and dischargeProfessionals decide follow-up under local procedures and record discharge from the service.

Disclaimer: Notifications are not life-critical alarms and are not intended to trigger immediate intervention.

Platform-supported workflows.

Supports virtual-ward workflows by enabling remote review and follow-up based on physiological data received from connected CE-marked sensors.

Outcomes depend on the clinical service model and local implementation.
