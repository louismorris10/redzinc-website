BlueEye First

Live video for emergency call centres

BlueEye First adds live video to an audio call, so 112 and 999 operators can assess a scene more quickly and make decisions more efficiently.

Request a trial

Why video matters in an emergency.

Time is critical. For stroke patients, time is brain; for heart patients, time is muscle. Standard call questioning takes time, and callers may struggle to describe what is happening, which can slow decisions. Real-time video with audio gives operators a direct view of the scene.

Key features.

 

Instant video call

Between the caller and the operator.

Live GPS location

Of the caller.

No download

No app or software for callers to install.

Secure and private

Secure, private video calls.

How it works

  1. The caller phonesThe caller phones the emergency centre and reaches an operator.
  2. The operator sends a linkThe operator texts the caller a secure link.
  3. The caller joins by videoThe caller opens the link and joins a video call with the operator.

Key outcomes

  • Better situation assessment and pre-hospital decision-making
  • Faster treatment and better patient outcomes
  • Time saved through quicker operator decisions
  • Greater efficiency in ambulance and hospital services

Secure data

BlueEye solutions comply with the GDPR and data protection regulations. All BlueEye video calls are AES-256 encrypted, and BlueEye uses TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 with secure ciphers only.

RedZinc is committed to regular penetration testing so that its solutions stay secure.
