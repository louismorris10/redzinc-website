BlueEye Handsfree

See what the wearer sees

BlueEye Handsfree is a wearable, mobile, point-of-view, cloud-based video service delivery platform. A wearable camera and a secure web portal support remote training, consultation and expert advice.

Request a demoRequest a free trial

What is in the kit.

Three parts work together.

Wearable headset

A lightweight headset with an HD camera, worn near the temple. It weighs 46 g and does not obstruct the wearer’s vision.

Smartphone

The headset connects to a smartphone, which carries the video over Wi-Fi, 4G or 5G.

Secure web portal

The remote audience watches live video, and listens and speaks, in any web browser.

Key features

  • A rotatable HD camera, so you choose the view
  • One-way video from the headset with two-way audio
  • Viewing in any web browser
  • Connectivity over Wi-Fi, 4G or 5G
  • Designed for busy healthcare settings

How it works

  1. Connect and wearConnect the headset to the smartphone and wear it near the temple.
  2. Start streamingStart streaming through the BlueEye app.
  3. Watch remotelyThe remote audience logs into the web portal to watch the live video.

Key outcomes

  • Less travel and lower associated costs
  • Greater convenience for everyone involved
  • Instant access to expert help
  • Fewer large groups gathering in one place

Secure data

BlueEye solutions comply with the GDPR and data protection regulations. All BlueEye video calls are AES-256 encrypted, and BlueEye uses TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 with secure ciphers only.

RedZinc is committed to regular penetration testing so that its solutions stay secure.

Frequently asked questions

How long does account setup take?
About 2 working days. Shipping takes about 5 working days in Europe and up to 10 working days outside Europe.
What does a subscription include?
Access to the software and upgrades, one headset, a smartphone with case, a docking clip, a lanyard and training materials.
What are the requirements?
A current web browser, a device with a camera and microphone where you are viewing, no organisational firewall block, and a connection with at least 5 Mbps download speed.
How do I get support?
Use the contact forms on this site or reach us on social media.
Can I use my own phone or camera?
The camera works only with the bundled smartphone and its pre-installed app.
Can I record video?
Upgrades are ongoing, but recording is not currently supported.
Are headsets available in different sizes?
Yes. Headsets come in different sizes for different neck sizes.
