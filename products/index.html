The BlueEye suite of products

RedZinc works with industry and academia worldwide to maintain a leading edge. We constantly innovate in web-based, real-time video communications and point-of-view wearable video.

BlueEye Virtual Ward

BlueEye Virtual Ward combines compatible connected sensors, a patient application and a clinician dashboard to support remote monitoring and virtual-ward workflows. It gives flexibility for remote and hands-on nurse resourcing, a video clinic service, and patient monitoring with non-life-critical review prompts and notifications, with an evidence-based approach to clinical governance.

Read more

BlueEye Handsfree

BlueEye Handsfree is a wearable, mobile, point-of-view, cloud-based video service delivery platform. It supports remote training, consultation or expert advice through live point-of-view video from a wearable camera and a secure web portal.

Read more

BlueEye First

Real-time video of an emergency scene benefits both the patient and the call centre. Live video with audio lets operators decide more quickly and efficiently.

Read more

BlueEye Clinic

BlueEye Clinic is a virtual clinic for healthcare professionals that recreates real-life consultations online. Use it in any clinic situation where a clinician or therapist speaks to a remote patient or client by video.

Read more

BlueEye Classroom

A 'See What I See' point-of-view view for remote medical students. Classroom brings point-of-view video to medical and nursing education.
