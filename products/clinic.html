BlueEye Clinic

Virtual clinic for healthcare professionals

A secure, easy-to-use video consultation platform to manage consultations with your patients and colleagues. Supports personal and common waiting rooms, role-based access, and instant, scheduled and recurring consultations.

Request a demoWatch video

BlueEye Clinic can also be used in community care settings to consult with a medical expert from the patient’s residence. Email support@blueeye.video to set up your account.

Key features.

 

No app download needed

Patients do not need to download an app or create an account.

Personal and common waiting rooms

Hold consultations in a personal waiting room or in an authorised common one.

Role-based access

Multiple user profiles and customisable role-based access fit different governance structures.

File transfer

Share relevant files and reports with other participants during the consultation.

Multi-party consultations

Group consultations with up to 30 participants.

Appointment management

A calendar to manage your video appointments with patients.

Three types of video call

  • Instantaneous call
  • Scheduled call
  • Drop-in call

Waiting rooms

  • Personal waiting room: a dedicated waiting area for a healthcare professional
  • Common waiting room: a waiting area accessible to multiple clinicians with authorised access

Types of accounts.

 

Clinician

Healthcare professional account for video consultations.

Clinical Manager

Personal assistant account to manage appointments on a clinician’s behalf.

Super Admin

Account manager account to manage users, access reports and analytics.

How it works

  1. Schedule the appointmentThe healthcare professional logs in to the BlueEye Clinic portal and schedules an appointment with a patient.
  2. Patient receives a secure linkFive minutes before the appointment, the patient receives a secure video link by text message and/or email.
  3. Patient enters the waiting roomThe patient clicks the link and enters the waiting room. The call connects when the professional joins.

Key outcomes

  • Improve operational efficiencies
  • See patients in their own environment
  • Eliminate geographical restrictions
  • Minimise travel
  • Eliminate contagion risk
  • Reduce your carbon footprint

What our customers say.

 

Secure data

BlueEye solutions comply with the GDPR and data protection regulations. All BlueEye video calls are AES-256 encrypted, and BlueEye uses TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 with secure ciphers only.

RedZinc is committed to regular penetration testing so that its solutions stay secure.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up a BlueEye Clinic account?
After the subscription, an account can be set up within 2 working days.
What is included in the subscription?
Login credentials for one BlueEye Clinic account, training material, a dedicated support system and access to future upgrades.
Can I record video calls?
No. BlueEye Clinic has no record function, which makes it more data secure for patients and healthcare professionals.
Do I need a special set-up or installation?
No. You need the latest browser on your computer, tablet or smartphone: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge or Safari.
Does the patient need any special set-up?
No. Patients do not download an app or software. They join from a desktop, laptop, tablet or smartphone using a current web browser.
What are the computer requirements?
  • Latest versions of a supported web browser and operating system.
  • A working camera (built in on most laptops, tablets and phones; a webcam on a desktop).
  • Browser access to the camera and microphone, which is requested when a call starts.
  • No organisational firewall blocking BlueEye Clinic.

Test your system before your first patient of the day using the Test Call function.

How can I get immediate support?
Each subscription includes a dedicated support system and training material. You can also use the contact forms on this site, or follow RedZinc on Twitter and LinkedIn for regular updates, tips and instructions.
Is patient information recorded or stored?
No. RedZinc does not store or record any information about patients who have attended BlueEye video calls.
Troubleshooting guide

Latest Google Chrome? Download or update the latest version, and check camera and microphone permissions in Chrome settings.

Latest Safari? Update from the App Store. To change camera and microphone access: Safari preferences → Websites → Camera and Microphone → Allow.

Many audio and video problems are resolved by clicking the Refresh button in the video.
