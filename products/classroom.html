BlueEye Classroom

See what I see: teaching from the clinician’s point of view

BlueEye Classroom combines a wearable camera with a cloud-based video service platform to deliver theoretical and practical training to remote students.

Request a demo

Medical and nursing education, without the crowd.

Demonstrate patient assessment and treatment using a point-of-view wearable camera. Remote students see the patient or procedure from the educator’s perspective, without gathering many people on a ward.

A moderator function handles scheduling, editing and administering live classes. The platform is described as easy to use, secure and GDPR compliant.

Benefits

  • Remote education for students anywhere in the world
  • A clear view of the procedure from the educator’s perspective
  • Less infection risk from large groups of students on the ward
  • No travel for students, saving cost and time
  • Medical schools can extend their reach to students abroad

Use cases.

Classroom use cases include virtual ward rounds, teaching medical students, surgery training, simulation training, nursing education and COVID isolation ward teaching.

Proven in Finland.

At Oulu University Hospital, medical education had stalled during the COVID-19 pandemic. The hospital used BlueEye wearable video to deliver paediatrics bedside tutorials to remote students. In a survey of 27 participants, more than 80% said teaching with BlueEye wearable video could partly or wholly replace traditional face-to-face teaching.
