Nursing education

Teach practical nursing skills to students anywhere.

About BlueEye ClassroomRequest a demo

The situation

Nurse education covers administering medicines, examining patients and delivering care across specialisations, through theory and practice. BlueEye Classroom combines a wearable camera with a cloud-based video platform, so remote students can see the patient or procedure from the educator's point of view.

How it works

  1. Educator wears the cameraA lightweight, ergonomic, portable camera streams point-of-view video.
  2. Students join remotelyRemote students follow urgent and emergency care, vital-sign checks and monitoring, patient education, patient safety and interpersonal skills.

Benefits

  • Remote nurse education for students anywhere in the world
  • A clear view of the procedure from the educator's perspective
  • No need to gather many students in one place
  • Cost savings and operational efficiency
  • Reduced contagion risk
