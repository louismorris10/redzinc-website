HSE support portal

Help for HSE clinicians using BlueEye Clinic.

About BlueEye Clinic

BlueEye Clinic is a web-based video consultation platform for healthcare providers. Clinicians can schedule, manage and conduct video consultations with patients. BlueEye Clinic is available to HSE clinicians, who set up accounts by emailing the support address.

Clinicians need

  • A BlueEye Clinic account
  • An up-to-date web browser
  • A computer with a camera and microphone
  • A reliable internet connection

Patients need

  • An up-to-date web browser on a mobile, tablet or computer
  • A camera, microphone and reliable internet
  • No account, app or special software

Training

Free BlueEye Clinic training for all HSE users is held on the last Tuesday of each month, with registration through a Zoom webinar link.

Features

  • Instant consultations: send a secure link by name, mobile number or email
  • Scheduled appointments
  • Group consultations with up to 20 participants, including the clinician
  • Reschedule, cancel or change participants on future appointments
  • Text chat and screen sharing
  • File transfer during a consultation

Resources

Guides are available for healthcare professionals and patients, including a Healthcare Professional Guide, an information leaflet, a Patient Troubleshooting Guide, a Patient Guide and three getting-started videos.
